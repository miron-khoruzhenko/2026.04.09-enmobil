# Enmobil Sigorta - General Information
- **Site Name**: Enmobil Sigorta (Company: ENMOBİL SİGORTA)
- **Sector**: Insurance (Sigorta) in Turkey
- **Target Audience**: Turkish citizens and businesses looking for quick, reliable insurance policies. Focus is on easy-to-use digital insurance experience (yeni nesil sigortacılık).
- **Key Products**: Kasko, Trafik Sigortası, Tamamlayıcı Sağlık, DASK, Konut, İşyeri, Özel Sağlık, Seyahat.
- **Theme Colors**: 
  - Red (`#D71D24`): Primary brand color, used for prominent actions and highlights.
  - Dark (`#1C1917`): Used for primary text and headings.
  - Light (`#F4F6F9`): Used for backgrounds, subtle containers.
  - Blue (`#0E71B8`): Secondary brand color.
- **Design Philosophy**: Modern, clean, and dynamic. Must avoid the "standard WordPress landing page" look. Needs premium feel with glassmorphism, micro-animations, and a highly accessible interface tailored to the Turkish market.

## Turkish Market Standards for Insurance:
- Prominent and quick access to "Teklif Al" (Get a Quote) forms right from the hero section.
- Clear categorization of individual (bireysel) and corporate (kurumsal) services.
- Contact options must be very accessible (WhatsApp, phone).
- Trust indicators (KVKK, policies, partnerships) are essential.
- Clear steps for the quote process.
